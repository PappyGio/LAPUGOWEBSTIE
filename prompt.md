Website Page

Main Function
Home
LapuGo introduction, tagline, featured destinations, highlighted tour packages, current promotions, travel highlights, and a brief overview of services
To create a strong first impression of LapuGo and immediately introduce visitors to the company’s travel experiences. The page will encourage users to explore Cebu and give them quick access to the destinations, packages, and services they may be interested in.
Destinations
Destination photos, descriptions, attractions, activities, travel information, recommended experiences, and nearby points of interest
To help travelers discover and learn more about Cebu’s destinations before choosing where to go. Users can explore different places based on the type of experience they want, such as beaches, island adventures, cultural attractions, and marine activities.
Tour Packages
Complete package details, itineraries, prices, duration, inclusions and exclusions, transportation, activities, and optional add-ons
To help customers understand and select the LapuGo experience that best fits their travel needs. The page will provide clear package information so travelers can compare options and determine which itinerary, activities, and budget are suitable for their group.
Services
Flight booking, visa and passport assistance, island hopping, yacht packages, land tours, marine activities, transportation assistance, and other travel services
To show that LapuGo provides more than tours by offering travel assistance before and during the customer’s journey. This page will help customers identify which services they need and understand how LapuGo can make their overall travel experience more convenient.
Gallery
Destination photographs, tour activities, island-hopping experiences, marine activities, customer experiences, and travel videos
To allow visitors to experience LapuGo visually before booking their trip. The gallery will showcase the actual destinations and activities offered by the company, helping customers visualize what their experience could look like.
Blog
Cebu travel guides, travel tips, suggested itineraries, destination recommendations, preparation tips, local information, and responsible tourism content
To provide useful information that helps travelers make informed and confident travel plans. The blog can also promote Cebu destinations and encourage responsible tourism by featuring tips related to environmental care and LapuGo’s Clean Shores initiative.
Contact
Company contact information, office location, social media accounts, inquiry form, booking request form, and other communication channels
To provide a direct connection between travelers and LapuGo. Customers can ask questions, request additional information, discuss their travel preferences, and submit booking or tour inquiries without having to personally visit the office.
